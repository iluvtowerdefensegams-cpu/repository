*Inspired by the Scratch learning platform*

- Details: Works in an orange gear-filled workshop. Unlocks programmable combat Robots (automaton summons with skill trees) and features a customization terminal that triggers a Deltarune-style "Design a machine to thrash your own ass" combat challenge.
