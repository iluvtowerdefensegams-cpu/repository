# Witch 
## Soul #29
 - **Role**: Weaver of the Woods / Hag of the Curse Dimension 
 - **Lore Summary**: The malevolent sorceress who traps minds in her 2D Curse Dimension. Slaying her yields Soul #29, reclaims PageStone #3, and unlocks Summoning Magic.
- **First Phrase**: "Oh, my poor little babies! Someone’s been a very, *very* naughty boy! Breaking into a lady's house and squishing her pets? Time for your punishment, little spider-crusher!"
- **Final Phrase**: "This... this isn't how the story... ends..."
