# Raid Boss
# Soul #10
### Role: First Boss / Leader of the Raiders (Soul #10)
### Profile & Narrative Arc:
Commands the bloodthirsty mercenary syndicate that torches your peaceful village in Chapter 2. Arrogant, heavy-handed, and ruthless, he confronts you in the burning town square after you cut down his initial squad (Souls 1–8). He mocks your youth and expects an easy kill, only to be overwhelmed by your dark power. Slaying him yields Soul #10 to the Dark Tome, cementing your first major victory and further trapping you in the Tome's unholy contract.
# First Phrase:
"So, you're the brat who's been killing my scouts?! I'm going to paint these walls with your guts!"
# Last Phrase:
"Gah... who... who the hell... is this kid...?"
