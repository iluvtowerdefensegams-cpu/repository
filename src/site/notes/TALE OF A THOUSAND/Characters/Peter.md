# Peter
## Soul #9
# Role: 
Childhood Best Friend / The First Sacrifice / The Ghost on Your Conscience
# Visual Appearance: 
Peter is a tall, slightly awkward village youth with messy brown hair and bright, earnest eyes. He wears simple linen clothes with leather elbow patches and carries a worn wooden practice sword at his hip. He always carries a scuffed leather satchel packed with library scrolls and strange trinkets he collects from local caves.
# Personality & Philosophy:
Peter is fiercely loyal, deeply empathetic, and naturally inquisitive. Unlike the player, who becomes hardened by survival, Peter represents pure, uncorrupted innocence. He believes in selflessness and the absolute sanctity of friendship. He is fascinated by history and arcane lore, but rejects the dark implications of magic, famously stating he could never bring himself to harm another human being to save himself. When the village falls, his immediate instinct is self-sacrifice—drawing his practice sword to buy the player time to reach the safety of the forest.
# Narrative Impact & Legacy:
Though Peter dies in Chapter 2 when the Dark Tome forcibly extracts his soul to "freeze the player's curse", his impact reverberates through the entire story:
1. **The PageStone Gift**: Before dying, Peter hands the player PageStone #1—a lucky charm he found in a cave that unlocks the Tome's spell matrix.
2. **The Emotional Catalyst**: His forced execution is the primary trauma that breaks the player's spirit, initiating their descent into cold detachment.
3. **The Resonant Echo**: Peter's final words (*"Don't forget who you are inside"*) become the moral tagline of the universe. They are later echoed word-for-word by Prince Mike in Chapter 4, serving as a constant reminder of what the player has traded away for power.
