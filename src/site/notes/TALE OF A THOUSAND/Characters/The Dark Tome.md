# The Dark Tome

## Overview
* **Name:** The Dark Tome
* **Soul:** None *(No soul — He is the Reaper / Sentient Catalyst)*
* **Role:** The Reaper / Sentient Catalyst / Main Antagonist
* **Alignment:** Absolute Evil / Deceptive Manipulator
* **Status:** Unbound / Unsealed (Re-bound to Skotos in Chapter 24)

---

## 🖤 Visual & Physical Description
* **Form:** An ancient, leather-bound grimoire encased in heavy iron trim with **NO eye**.
* **Physical State:** Strapped to the host's waist or carried in hand during the journey. Does NOT float (hovering only occurs when unsealed in the epilogue).
* **Voice:** A dark, tempestuous command—strong, fierce, imposing, and unrelenting. Whispers directly inside the host's mind. (Sometimes yells though)

---

## 😈 True Nature & Alignment: Is He a Bad Guy?
**Yes, the ultimate bad guy.**
* **The Deception:** At the start of the story (Chapter 1), the Tome acts as a "savior," offering to freeze the fatal curse in your veins in exchange for a simple soul pact.
* **The Manipulation:** Throughout your journey across the continent, he rationalizes every murder, encouraging you to cut down enemies, allies, and innocent bystanders alike by promising power, freedom, and revenge.
* **The Betrayal:** He never intended to save you. As the Reaper, he holds no soul of his own and requires **100 pure souls** to unseal Skotos. After you harvest Soul #99 (Prince Mike), the Tome unbuckles itself from your waist and extracts **YOUR** soul as Soul #100.

---

## 📜 Powers & Dark Capabilities
1. **Soul Extraction (The Reaper's Touch):** Instantly siphon and store the life force of fallen foes into its pages upon defeat.
2. **Curse Suppression:** Freezes metabolic decay, injuries, or the fatal curse in your blood in exchange for complete soul compliance.
3. **Dark Set Synchronization:** Enables the host to wield the Living Dark Sword, Dark Armour, and Dark Wings.
4. **Mind Whispering:** Constant psychological conditioning—eroding the host's morality, empathy, and hesitation over time.

## 🎬 Narrative Arc Across Chapters
* **Chapters 1–3 (The Hook):** Freezes your fatal curse and guides you through harvesting your first 10 souls (Raiders & Raid Boss).
* **Chapters 4–15 (The Descent):** Teaches you to stop hesitating. Frames pirates, witches, and bandits as simple fuel for the Dark Set.
* **Chapters 16–23 (The Harvesting Engine):** Silences your guilt as you slaughter guards, heroes, and even your friend's allies in the Capital.
* **Chapter 24 (The Unsealing):** Claims your father (Soul #98) and Prince Mike (Soul #99). Immediately betrays you, extracting your soul as **Soul #100** to complete Skotos' resurrection.
