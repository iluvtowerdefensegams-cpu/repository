# Raiders
## Souls 1-8
### Leader: [[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Raid Boss\|Raid Boss]] (A ruthless, battle-scarred bandit chieftain)
## # Role: The Initial Catalyst / Moral Trap
## 🩸 Description & Background
A ruthless mercenary syndicate of brutal pillagers and opportunistic cutthroats who prowl the countryside, attacking peaceful settlements. Driven purely by greed and mindless violence, they torch wooden homes, butcher unarmed villagers, and plunder anything of value. Armed with torch, cutlass, and crude armor, they operate without honor or mercy.
## 🎭 Narrative Significance & Moral Hook
- The "Justified" Slaughter: When the Raiders swarm your home village in Chapter 2, you are forced to draw your sword and cut down 8 of them to survive.
- The Tome's Gateway Drug: Because the Raiders are remorseless, bloodthirsty killers, taking their lives feels completely justified at first. The Dark Tome uses your righteous anger against them to make harvesting their souls feel natural—lulling you into accepting the soul-harvesting contract.
- The Escalation: Once you become comfortable taking the 8 Raiders' souls, the Tome gradually escalates its demands to ambiguous targets (bandits, hexed explorers, pirates) and ultimately to innocent lives (Peter, townspeople, Dad).
