# Tyme
⏳ 1. The Lore & Identity of Tyme
Who is he?
He is a Chronological Anomaly—a dark reflection of what happens when a "chosen one" (like Mike or the protagonist) survives beyond their intended narrative. In his dead timeline, after a millennium of slaughtering horrors to survive, the timeline collapsed into a closed loop. He didn't die; he just outlived his world until he became one with the ticking clock itself.
His Philosophy on "Time Wasted":
Every kill, every restart, every side detour you took wasn't just blood spilled—it was seconds stolen from the universe. When you killed someone, you cut their clock short and bloated your own.

His iconic battle cry:
"Every heartbeat you cut short was time you stole to fuel your own rampage. I will be taking my time back."
