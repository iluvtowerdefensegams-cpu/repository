### ⚒️ Smithy Steve (The Forge)
*Inspired by Minecraft's Steve*
- **Role**: The Blocky Forge Master / Explosive Craftsman (Minecraft Steve inspired)
- **Status**: Alive (No Soul / Forge Exemption)
- **Details**: A blocky, muscular blacksmith who yearned for the mines as a child. Upgrades swords and bows with mined ores and gunpowder. Upgrade sequences trigger loud TNT explosions that leave him completely unfazed.
"I... am Steve. As a child, I yearned for the mines. The deep, dark blocks called to me. Now, I shape the metal." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Steve The BlackSmith-Smithy Steve\|Steve The BlackSmith-Smithy Steve]]

(Also he exists to recreate the Minecraft memes)
