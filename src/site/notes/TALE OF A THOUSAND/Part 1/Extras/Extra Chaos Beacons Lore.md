# Extra Chaos Beacons Lore
Chaos beacons are fragments of the Central Beacon. When a soul becomes attached to a beacon, their story synchronizes.
+ Septer was the first person to channel energy out of a beacon, and he used it for his main purpose in life: To Spread The Fun. Things went wrong when he replaced all human workers with automated workers.
+ The big city companies saw the potential of the beacons in a monetary way and took advantage of it, seeing the resulting slave civilization as simply "more money".
+ They set up a nuclear factory near the desert to feed the Capital's power grid. While the Capital basked in lazy comfort, the factory workers starved on unpaid wages, wasting what little they had on the Capital's addictive products. They grew desperate—stealing fuel and rioting—yet none ever dared strike at the corporate heads in the Capital. How could they, when the whole world was addicted to the system?
+ Someone knew how to fix it. Someone knew, that they had to cut their strings. But they couldn't surpass The One Fooling Them All. Her body was turned into a prison itself, but her soul remained ever-free... Or did it? Teeheehee!
