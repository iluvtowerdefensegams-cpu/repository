- "Puppet Funergy" Team Combo: When Puppy uses her spectral strings to lock an elite enemy in place, Scepter can channel a high-voltage Funergy Beam through the strings, creating a chain-lightning explosion across all linked wooden puppets!
- Companion Banter: Add cute ambient dialogue between Scepter (who is loud, energetic, and carnival-themed) and Puppy (who is tiny, timid, and string-themed) floating at your shoulders during exploration.
- Optional Speedrun Medal / Trophy: If the player clears the Gauntlet in under 90 seconds without taking damage, the Dark Tome makes a snarky meta comment ("Damn, you've been grinding! Are you speedrunning this story?") and awards a secret cosmetic item: The Runner's Stop Watch.
## Post Chapter 14 Difficulty Raise:
- Aggressive AI: Enemies no longer wait their turn to attack—they swarm, flank, and use anti-heal moves.
- Red "Malice" Stagger Meters: Elite enemies get an enraged "Malice Shield" that requires heavy Dark Sword combo breaks or Puppy/Scepter companion abilities to shatter.
- Corrupted Weather: Re-entering the Dark Forest in Chapter 15 isn't peaceful anymore—it’s bathed in a blood-red haze with aggressive shadow variants of early-game mobs.
