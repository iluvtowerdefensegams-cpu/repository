# The Four Vessels of the Grimoire

## Act I: The First Vessel — The Broken Courier

When Skotos was first shattered across the mortal plane, Lampros’ surviving servants made a solemn pledge: the pieces of the Dark Set must never be reunited.
- A venerable High Priest took the Dark Armour to her secluded sanctuary high within the frozen mountain peaks.
- The wizard Wignus locked the Dark Sword away inside the high chambers of his arcane tower.
- A veteran Warrior, the set’s original bearer, volunteered to carry the Dark Tome to his homeland and bury it deep underground.  

He never reached his destination. While traversing the dense canopy of the Dark Forest, the dormant malice within the leather binding awakened. Strange, glowing violet runes burned across the warrior’s arms—the first manifestation of the curse in recorded history. When the glyphs ignited, he was given three minutes to offer a living sacrifice or perish. Refusing to spill innocent blood, the warrior collapsed in the brush, suffocated by the curse’s unyielding clock.

When Wignus discovered the courier's demise, he realized the grimoire was far too dangerous to leave unguarded. He assembled a vanguard of fifteen champions to venture into the forest and subdue the rampant Tome. After a grueling battle against the wood’s dark abominations, they overpowered the relic. To cripple its might, each of the fifteen champions tore out a single page to scatter across the realm. Wignus then cast a crushing seal upon the book’s core, shattering its self-sustaining essence.

From that day forth, the Tome was rendered hollow: unable to sustain its own magic without feeding on the souls of mortals.

## Act II: The Second Vessel — The Wimpy Traveler

Centuries passed as the Tome lay dormant beneath decaying leaves. Eventually, an unsuspecting traveler stumbled upon the book. Sensing a pulse of fresh blood, the Tome branded his arm with the curse, coercing the terrified man into its service.

Yet this vessel was an utter coward. Paralyzed by fear, he spent weeks in hiding, trembling at every shadow. In total, he managed to harvest only five meager souls—three road bandits and two docks cutthroats.

With five souls pooled in its pages, the Tome had enough reserve energy to tether another body. Tired of harboring a pathetic weakling, the grimoire turned its gaze toward a far deadlier tool.

## Act III: The Third Vessel — The Ruthless Conqueror

A battle-hardened warrior soon crossed paths with the terrified traveler. Sensing the warrior's ambition, the Tome flared into his mind, offering him its boundless power if he culled the wimpy vessel on the spot. Without an ounce of hesitation, the warrior cut the traveler down, reaping his soul as his very first offering.

This third vessel proved terrifyingly efficient. He amassed souls, reclaimed five of the fifteen lost pages, and set out to assemble the Dark Set:

- **The Dark Armour:** He scaled the frozen northern peaks and stormed the mountain sanctuary. There, he murdered the venerable High Priest—the beloved mentor and teacher of the Cleric. Stripping the obsidian plates from her corpse, he claimed the armour.
- **The Dark Sword:** He marched on Wignus Tower. Finding the ancient wizard completely distracted and unattended in his private chambers while taking a bath, the intruder slipped into the armory unnoticed and took the blade without a clash of steel.

Armed with the Sword, Armour, and Tome, Vessel #3 marched toward the Capital to achieve absolute dominion. But within the deep fog of the Dark Forest, he was intercepted by three servants of Lampros: a Warrior, the wizard Wignus, and the High Priest’s successor—the Cleric (your Mother).

The battle was devastating. Realizing their steel and spells could not pierce the assembled dark armor, the Cleric fell to her knees, making the ultimate choice. Using the last reserves of her divine power, she healed her two wounded companions, passed her sealed letter to the Warrior for her family (*"HURRY! Take this to my house!"*), and commanded Wignus to flee to the Capital to warn the realm.

Once her allies escaped into the trees, the Cleric stood alone before the dark conqueror. As he stepped forward to execute her, she unleashed a blinding, sacrificial detonation of pure divine light. 

The holy blast froze Vessel #3 solid on the spot, shattering his physical body into clouds of dust. 

Before the light consumed her mortal frame, the Cleric lunged forward into the ash, seized the dropped Dark Tome, and desperately tore out all five of the pages the warlord had recovered, scattering them back into the woods. A split-second later, her remaining life force erupted into a towering pillar of light. Where her soul dissolved into the forest soil, a single glowing blossom took root in the ash: **The Orchid of Power**.

### The Aftermath & The Banishment

Having delivered the warning to the Capital as ordered, Wignus returned to the quiet, smoking clearing in the forest.

Among the ash, he found the shattered remains of the battle. He recovered the Dark Sword to lock away in his tower once more. To ensure the remaining artifacts could never again be brought together by a single hand, Wignus gathered his arcana and cast a massive displacement spell across the clearing.

The spell violently banished the artifacts to opposite ends of the continent:
- **The Dark Armour** was cast into the desolate northern heights, buried deep beneath an impenetrable glacier of ice.
- **The Dark Tome** was flung across the tree line, hurtling into a hidden subterranean cavern merely a mile from the Cleric's home village.

## Act IV: The Fourth Vessel — The Child (YOU)

Unbeknownst to the world, the grimoire lay trapped in that underground hollow for years... until raiders set the village ablaze, your marks burst into violet flame, and the 3-minute timer began to tick.
