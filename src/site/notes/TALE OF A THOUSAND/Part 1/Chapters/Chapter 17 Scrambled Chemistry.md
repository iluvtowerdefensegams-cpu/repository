# Chapter 17: Scrambled Chemistry
The air transitions from the damp forest air into a suffocating, lime-green haze. You pull Salariem’s heavy gas mask over your face, the mechanical filter clicking into place just as the environment shifts into a toxic, bubbling mire.

(Muffled Audio & Vignette: Equipping the Gas Mask as you enter the Swamps adds a subtle muffled breathing sound effect and a protective green-tinted visor HUD overlay, shielding your HP from the toxic fog!)

Tucked into the roots of a giant, dying willow tree is a ramshackle wooden laboratory covered in glowing flasks and copper pipes. You don't burst down the door; you just push it open.
Inside, there’s no dramatic music. A man covered in stained alchemist aprons and heavy rubber gloves is casually stirring a boiling vat, hummed text boxes floating over his head. This is **Pike the Poisoner**. He doesn't even look up at first.

"Filter's on tight, I see. Good. Don't touch the glass on the left, it'll melt your boots off," -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Pike The Poisoner\|Pike The Poisoner]]

He glances at the glowing Orchid of Power in your hand and chuckles, wiping his hands on a rag.

"Ah, Mary’s flower. Guess she's pushing up her own daisies now, huh? Fine, toss it in the distiller. I'll teach you how to refine it. You want to survive the wastes, you need to know how to brew a proper neurotoxin anyway." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Pike The Poisoner\|Pike The Poisoner]]
### 🧪 The Lesson & The Backstory
Instead of an immediate fight, Pike actually guides your hand. The UI changes to a crafting menu as you follow his instructions, crushing the petals and balancing the acidic levels. As the potion bubbles into a dark, volatile violet vial, Pike idly starts talking strategy.

"You know, a lot of idiots think poison is just about killing. It’s about leverage. Take the Raiders, for instance. Their old leader thought he could muscle me for free stock. So, I gave him exactly what he wanted—highly customized, 'specialized' combat supplies. Two days later, his whole camp was too paralyzed to pick up their swords. Funny how a little scam keeps people in line. Speaking of scams..." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Pike The Poisoner\|Pike The Poisoner]]

Pike corks the finished potion and sets it on the table between you.

"Who's this brew actually for anyway?" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Pike The Poisoner\|Pike The Poisoner]]

*"Salariem."* -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/You\|You]]

The room goes dead silent. The faint humming from the boiling vats suddenly feels incredibly loud. Pike’s casual posture completely vanishes. His eyes drop to the vial, then slowly trail up to the dark, pulsing runes on your arm.

"Salariem." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Pike The Poisoner\|Pike The Poisoner]]

He lets out a low, venomous laugh that rattles inside his throat.

"That absolute rat. He owes me a fortune in raw materials, and instead of paying, he sends some kid to my doorstep. He didn't send you to pick up a delivery, kid. He sent you to clear his tab." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Pike The Poisoner\|Pike The Poisoner]]

Before you can even draw your sword, Pike slams his fist onto a giant red lever on the wall.
*CLANG!* Heavy, rusted iron shutters violently slam down over the doors and windows. The copper pipes overhead snap open, flooding the floor with a thick, shimmering purple gas.

"He wants me dead? Fine. But I'm locking the cage, and neither of us is leaving this room alive!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Pike The Poisoner\|Pike The Poisoner]]
### 🤢 BOSS BATTLE: Pike The Poisoner
### 🌀 MECHANICS: Total Sensory Distortions
This isn't a clean arena fight. It's a claustrophobic, panicked struggle inside a small room filling with neurotoxins.
 * **Scrambled Controls:** The purple gas instantly bypasses your mask. Your physical keyboard keys randomly swap every 15 seconds. Pressing W might make you move left, while Spacebar suddenly triggers your inventory. You have to constantly adapt to the chaotic UI shifts on the fly. (Btw to not make it impossible the HUD shows what each button does and WASD only change with WASD (So like w is left d is up a is right a is down))
 * **The Confusion Status:** Pike doesn't fight honorably. He throws volatile acid flasks that blind your screen and creates mirror-image illusions of himself in the fog.
 * **The Ticking Clock:** The longer the fight goes, the more your max HP permanently caps down due to the toxic fumes. You have to hunt the real Pike down through the panic before you suffocate.
You push through the absolute chaos of your scrambled movements, predicting a lunging strike from the fog, and drive your blade straight through Pike's chest, throwing him crashing backward into his own alchemy shelf.
Dozens of volatile flasks shatter over him, his body instantly dissolving into a violent, chemical reaction alongside a burst of dark energy.

"Salariem... you... brilliant... bastard..." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Pike The Poisoner\|Pike The Poisoner]]

From the chemical ash, a glowing green perk token floats into your inventory: **[POISON BREWING UNLOCKED]**. Alongside it, you snatch the finalized violet potion from the counter.

As Pike's shelves collapse, a sealed, antique flask filled with thick, iridescent luminescent reagent rolls off the top rack and shatters against the stone floor. As the volatile liquids react with the sulfur and dark ash, the boiling puddle begins to rapidly calcify, hardening and transmuting into a crisp sheet of parchment preserved in the center of the chemical circle.

### 📜 SPECIAL ITEM RECOVERY: The Tale — Page 5 (The Gluttony of Ghosts)
*The parchment radiates intense, unyielding alchemical heat, its edges glowing with a dying amber reaction. The gothic calligraphy is bordered by illustrations of a fracturing porcelain vessel overflowing with screaming, silhouetted spirits.*

> *"And what stirs the heart of the Calamity as he strides through the weeping ruins of the earth?
> 
> The heralds whisper that he marches under the lash of survival—a desperate beast fleeing the venom in his own veins. Others murmur that he wanders in search of a lost bond, tearing the sky apart to reclaim blood stolen by the dark.
> 
> Yet let the stars witness the grim truth: noble causes are merely the soil in which atrocities take root. For when the blade grows warm and the tally mounts into the twilight, the distinction between salvation and slaughter dissolves. 
> 
> A mortal frame was never forged to be a pantheon. To swallow fifty human souls is an act of unfathomable greed—a gluttony of stolen lifeforces that tears the spirit from the bone. The vessel will swell, the mind will rot, and the flesh will splinter beneath the sheer weight of its stolen ghosts.
> 
> He does not butcher because he must. In the quiet chambers of his hollow breast... he butchers because the silence answers him."*
> 
> *(Scrawled across the bottom margin in The Writer's aggressive pencil is another heavily scribbled-out note. On a first playthrough, it is thoroughly blacked out by thick graphite and completely unreadable. Only upon completing the game does the handwriting decrypt):*
> 
> *[NEW GAME+ / ARCHIVE UNLOCK]*
> *Draft Note: The tragedy of the protagonist isn't that he started evil—it's that he uses his father as moral permission. 'I'm doing this to find my dad' becomes his psychological shield while he harvests fifty innocent souls. The physical cracking of his body at Soul 50 is the world physically manifesting his greed. The player should feel that sickening doubt: Is he still saving his family, or does he just love holding the sword?*

*(The Dark Tome lets out a low, raspy chuckle at your hip, its pages fluttering lazily in the toxic mist)*
"Survival? Family? The greed of fifty souls? Ha! Look at the pompous philosopher who penned this melodrama, trying to dissect our appetite. Who cares if the jar cracks, so long as the power is ours? Snap it in your binder, partner." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/The Dark Tome\|The Dark Tome]]

*(You gaze down at the steaming page through your gas mask. The words 'lost bond' and 'greed' sting against the raw, numb void in your chest, but your eyes remain cold and resolute.)*

*(You unclamp The Tale Binder, clicking the steel rings open to lock Page 5 securely into place alongside the others before snapping the brass clasp shut.)*

"A monster gets the job done." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/You\|You]]

A glowing purple soul rises from the ash, surging directly into your blade. **Soul Count: 50!**
Suddenly, a violent, agonizing pain shoots through your chest. Your character drops to one knee, coughing harshly as dark, jagged cracks erupt across the skin of your arms and neck!

*(The Dark Tome shudders violently at your hip, its pages slamming open and shut in frantic, genuine panic)*
"PARTNER! STOP! LISTEN TO ME RIGHT NOW!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/The Dark Tome\|The Dark Tome]]

*Clenching your teeth as violet light bleeds through the cracks in your skin, forcing your fingers to wrap tighter around the hilt* "They... won't break me..." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/You\|You]]

"Your body is cracking, you fool! Fifty souls! A mortal frame cannot contain this much concentrated energy without shattering to dust! Your mind and body will be pulverized in minutes if we keep pushing forward like this!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/The Dark Tome\|The Dark Tome]]

*(The glowing purple eye on the Dark Sword's guard flares with a sharp, pulsing light, pointing its tip directly toward the frozen peaks above)*
"Master... I sense it... High above... encased in ancient, unyielding ice... **The Dark Armour**! We must reach it before your vessel gives out!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/The Dark Sword\|The Dark Sword]]

You force yourself back to your feet, gritting your teeth as the dark runes pulse under your skin, holding your cracking form together just long enough to move.

The iron shutters automatically screech upward. With the toxic room clearing, you step out into the forest air, holding the exact leverage Salariem wanted—and fully aware that the shopkeeper just tried to use you as a weapon.

You step back through the heavy pneumatic doors of Lyze HQ, the hum of servers feeling a lot colder this time. You don't even look at the other stations. You walk straight up to the inventory counter, your heavy boots thudding against the metal floor.
You slam the violet potion down onto the counter. You don't say a word. Your face isn't angry—it's just dead, empty, and radiating a clear message: *I'm about to take your soul.*
Salariem looks at the potion, then slowly looks up at you. A drop of sweat visibly rolls down his cheek, but he instantly forces his signature wide, theatrical grin, raising his hands in defense.

*"Aha! Back so soon, my dark, intimidating friend! And look at that, the potion is perfectly brewed! Splendid! Truly splendid! ...Hey, uh, why are you looking at me like you're calculating my exact market value in souls? Surely we're all friends here, right? Just a little misunderstanding!"* -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Salariem The Shopkeeper\|Salariem The Shopkeeper]]

You rest your hand on the hilt of the Dark Tome's blade. The eye on the crossguard snaps open, staring directly into Salariem's soul.

*The pages flip with a dangerous, slow hum.*
"He thinks he's a director, partner. He thought he could write us into his little script. Shall we tear him out of the book?" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/The Dark Tome\|The Dark Tome]]

Salariem ducks under the counter out of sheer panic and quickly shoves a heavy, glowing electronic component and a bundle of thick, insulated fabric across the metal.

*"No need for violence! Look, here is the Antenna Synchronization Part! Exactly as promised! And hey, since you did such a... spectacular job cleaning up the local wetland ecology, let's call it a bonus—here is the military-grade Warm Gear! Free of charge! Fits perfectly under any dark, edgy armor!"* -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Salariem The Shopkeeper\|Salariem The Shopkeeper]]

​You reach across the counter, slowly sweeping the antenna part and thermal suit into your pack, leaning in close until your cold breath hits his face

"Next time you write a script for me, Salariem... make sure I die in it. Because if I survive, you won't." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/You\|You]]

​Salariem swallows hard, nodding rapidly as you turn on your heel

​"A wise choice, partner. Let the rat breathe a little longer. We have a peak to scale." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/The Dark Tome\|The Dark Tome]]

Leaving Salariem sighing with immense relief behind his counter, you turn on your heel. You pull the heavy thermal suit over your clothes, feeling the biting cold of the world instantly bounce off the fabric.
You march back out through the sliding doors, stepping back into the shadows of the forest shortcut, and begin your trek straight back up toward the steep, freezing borders of the mountain peak.
