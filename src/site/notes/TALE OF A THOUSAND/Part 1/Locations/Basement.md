[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Part 1/Locations/Your House\|Your House]]
Environment: A damp, dust-covered stone cellar beneath your house, lit by thin golden rays of sunlight filtering through the floorboards above (and later, choked with smoke during Chapter 2).

### Key Points of Interest & Items:
- The Family Lockbox Chest: A heavy oak chest bound in iron. Contains your family's Steel Sword (a sturdy blade forged by Dad in his youth) and a leather pouch of travel coins and medical wraps.
- Mom's Hidden Lampros Crest: Tucked behind an old woven tapestry on the back stone wall is a faint, carved symbol of Lampros (a radiant sunburst with twin feathers). Touching it makes your curse marks hum with a cool, soothing warmth—causing the Dark Tome to hiss in annoyance ("Ugh... that nauseating stench of divine light... ignore it, kid!").
- Dad's Woodworking Bench: Scattered with carpentry chisels, wood shavings, and half-finished wooden practice toys Dad carved for you and Peter when you were little.
- Storage Stash: Preserved food jars, a brass oil lantern, extra linen blankets, and a splintered practice shield from childhood games.
