# Chapter 4: The Pirate Docks
The soft grass of the green fields slowly gives way to damp wood, fog, and the biting stench of saltwater. As you walk along the pier toward the main harbor, a glint of gold catches your eye. You notice a piece of highly intricate, royal-looking jewelry resting on Mike’s socks.

"What's that?" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/You\|You]]

"Oh, this? Nothing much! Just an old family heirloom... Hey, I've been meaning to ask, Not-My-Friend—what kind of business do you actually have at the docks?" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Mike\|Mike]]

"I'm looking for... acquisitions. Valuable things." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/You\|You]]

*(Whispering harshly from your hip)* "Things that fit inside my nose!!!!!! Please find me a crayon 🥺 😔." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/The Dark Tome\|The Dark Tome]]

"Oh, wow! So you're looking for treasure to buy stuff? That's so cool! You should have just said so. Here, you can have some of mine!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Mike\|Mike]]

Mike beams and reaches into his pockets, pulling out a handful of heavy, glittering gold coins. But the moment he extends his hand to give them to you, a shadowy figure darts out from an alleyway, snatches the pouch right out of his grip, and sprints down the pier.

"Hey! Wait! That wasn't very nice!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Mike\|Mike]]

You sprint after the thief, cornering him at the edge of the lower docks. Suddenly, five heavily armed bandits step out from behind the shipping crates, completely blocking your escape. Standing in the center is their leader, a ruthless-looking man with a massive scarred jaw, alongside the [[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Captain\|Captain]].

"Well, well. Look what we have here. A couple of stray fish swimming right into the net. *Sniffs* *turns nose 360°* Oh, we definitely got us some dogfish." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Captain\|Captain]]

"Excuse me, sir! Your friend back there took our coins. They aren't yours, so please give them back!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Mike\|Mike]]

*Looks at the gold pouch, then bursts into a booming laugh* "Too bad, kid. Everything that touches these docks belongs to us now." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Captain\|Captain]]

The Captain turns his back on you, casually tossing the pouch to one of his men as he begins to walk away.

"[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Jaw\|Jaw]]." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Captain\|Captain]]

"Yes, sir?" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Jaw\|Jaw]]

"Get rid of them. Quietly. And make sure to clean up after. I don't wanna smell the docks from my ship." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Captain\|Captain]]

*Drawing a heavy cutlass* "Aye aye, Captain!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Jaw\|Jaw]]
## ⚔️ ALLY ENCOUNTER: The Ambush at the Docks
The Arena triggers. The atmosphere instantly shifts from the bright fields to a gritty, high-stakes battleground.
 * **Party Status:** Mike is an active combatant! Because you are fighting actual "bad guys" who attacked first, Mike will **not** block your attacks here. Instead, he fights alongside you as a proper Party Member.
 * **Enemies:** You must defeat **4 Pirates** and the mini-boss, **Jaw**.
   The final pirate falls. Jaw collapses against a stack of cargo crates, gripping his shattered armor, completely defeated.
"I failed you, Cap... Please... just take my life and get it over with. Please. I can't stand this smell of defeat..." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Jaw\|Jaw]]

*Steps in front of you, sheathing his sword with absolute sincerity* "We would never do that! Even if you stole from us and fought against us, we have no intention of killing you. Go home, mister." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Mike\|Mike]]

*Blinks in disbelief, coughing up sea salt* "Ah... a heart of gold, that is." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Jaw\|Jaw]]

*(Whispering furiously into your mind)* "Hehehe I want a Jaw up my nose. Mommy! Bring me my food!!!!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/The Dark Tome\|The Dark Tome]]

(You glance at Mike, then down at your burning curse marks. You swallow hard.)
"Hey, Mike? Where exactly did the Captain run off to?" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/You\|You]]

"Oh! I don't know! I'll go scout ahead and look for him!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Mike\|Mike]]

Mike enthusiastically sprints down the pier, turning the corner out of sight. The moment he leaves, the warm ambient lighting instantly drops into a cold, shadowy hue. You turn back to Jaw, your face twisting into a cold expression.

*Struggling to back away, terror filling his eyes* "W-what are ye doing? Please! Keep your distance, you smell!! " -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Jaw\|Jaw]]

You raise your arm, your curse marks flaring with a violent purple light. The screen cuts to green for a split second as a slow, brutal farting sound plays, followed by the heavy, echoing sound of a scent being violently sniffed away.
The camera snaps back. Jaw lies dead on the wooden planks. Quickly, your character puts him in a barrel and kicks it, pushing it off the pier. *SPLASH.*
Right on cue, Mike comes running back around the corner, entirely out of breath.

"Hey, Not-My-Friend! I couldn't find—oh! What was inside that barrel you just kicked into the sea?" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Mike\|Mike]]

"Rotten fish. It smelled terrible." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/You\|You]]

*Sniffs the salty, smoky air, completely buying it* "*in a low voice* I'm not really sure that's what smelled... Phew! Glad you got rid of it! Anyway, I finally located the Captain. See that massive galleon anchored way out over there? That's his flagship. But... how are we going to reach it? The water is way too deep to swim. Actually it might not be a bad idea to dip in the water for a bit." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Mike\|Mike]]

"NOOOOO WATER NOO IT STINGS IT BURNS NO NO NO NO WATER NO TOUCHY ME" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/The Dark Tome\|The Dark Tome]] (btw touching water insta-kills you)

You look around the harbor and spot a small, unassuming rowboat tied to the lower dock.

"We can take this ship." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/You\|You]]

"Hey, good idea! Let's set sail!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Mike\|Mike]]

You both hop into the small vessel.
*(As you take the oars in the small rowboat, surrounded by sea fog)*

"So... we're rowing straight into a pirate fleet in a wooden bathtub," -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Mike\|Mike]] *says, looking at you with a grin.* "If we're about to get blown to bits, Not-My-Friend, don't you think I deserve to know your real name?"

*(You stare into the water for a long moment, reluctant)*
"...`<username>`." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/You\|You]]
"See? That wasn't so hard! `<username>`... I like it! Fits a true stinker. ---I mean hero! Hero! Not stinker! Hahahah! Now, uhm... please let *me* handle the rowing." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Mike\|Mike]]

Instead of standard rowing, you use the Tome's smelly magic to repel the water behind the boat, steering the small boat directly into a chaotic naval skirmish.
## ⛵ Minigame: The Foggy Ship Battle
 * **Controls:** Steer the rowboat using WASD or Arrow Keys.
 * **Combat:** Left-click to fire concentrated bursts of [[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Part 1/Spells/Sweat Blast\|Sweat Blast]] from the bow of your boat.
 * **Hazards:** Dodge incoming, massive cannonballs from the Captain's flagship and the water splashes.
 * **Power-ups:** Collect floating debris for temporary buffs: **Rapid Fire**, **Mega Blast** (High Damage), **Dark Shield**, and **Spread Shot**.
   The flagship's health bar hits 0. The massive galleon shudders, taking on water, and sinks heavily onto a shallow sandbar, its top deck remaining entirely above water. You and Mike leap onto the deck.
"You bastards! You sunk me ship! You will pay with your blood!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Captain\|Captain]]
## ⚔️ CLIMAX BATTLE: The Captain of the Docks
 * **Encounter:** Fight the Captain on the tilting, slippery wooden deck.
 * **Reward:** Upon defeating him, he drops his weapon (unlocking a better sword for you) and the [[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Equipment/Armours/Captain's Hat\|Captain's Hat]] armor piece.
   The Captain falls to his knees, utterly defeated.
"Argh... I lost to a couple of street rats... *Smelly* street rats..." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Captain\|Captain]]

"Yay! We stopped them and saved the docks! Come on, let's go find the stolen treasure!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Mike\|Mike]]

A massive wave crashes into the shallow reef, breaking the ship apart. The hull grinds onto the shore of a nearby beach. Mike immediately runs toward a large, ornate chest that washed up on the sand.
## The Hidden Harvest
While Mike is entirely distracted by the chest, you turn to the struggling Captain. You raise your arm. Your curse marks flare violently.

"W-wait! Please—!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Captain\|Captain]]

You deliver a swift, lethal *strike*. The Captain instantly falls unconscious. *Big sniffy sniff sniff*. You use your boot to violently kick the body off the ship's shattered hull and into the crashing ocean waves.
You step down onto the beach, heading toward the treasure chest. But right before you can touch it, Mike steps directly into your path, blocking your hand. His usual bright smile is completely gone.

"What did you do?" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Mike\|Mike]]

"I cleared the path." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/You\|You]]

"You killed him. I saw you." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Mike\|Mike]]

"He was a pirate. He would have hunted us down." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/You\|You]]

*A single tear falls down his cheek, his voice trembling but firm* "That doesn't give you the right to play executioner! You can't just go around slaughtering everyone in sight! I sense the frustration, the anger, and the deep depression twisting inside you, `<username>`. But you don't have to let it control you! You can choose who to be. No matter where you are, or what you do... *You should learn to shower!*- I MEAN- UHHH OKAY NEVERMIND YOU GOT MY POINT" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Mike\|Mike]]

Your character takes a step back, drawing their weapon defensively.

*Draws his traveler's blade, a brilliant light emanating from the steel* "Very well then. If you're going to let that stinkiness completely consume you...
# I shall be the one to stop you.
"-[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Mike\|Mike]]
## ⚔️ BOSS BATTLE: Mike, hero of the tides 
 * **The Reality:** Mike is utilizing most of his strength now (70%). Armed with incredible stats and fast, devastating parries, **he absolutely kicks your ass.** This is an overwhelming battle designed to show his true potential and vigilante resolve. (But it's not a scripted loss). This version is much harder. There are attacks where the water will rise. If you so much as touch water you instantly die. Mike also does some occasional water blasts.
#### Death Screen & Time Rewind Lore: Added canonical lore for defeat during Mike's boss fight (and game deaths in general).
- Standard deaths display the Tome asking: "Dude, what a stinker, we lost. Wanna try again?"
- Specifically when defeated by Mike, the Dark Tome intervenes before Mike can seal you: "EW EW EW WATER IT STINGSSSSS!" rewinding time back to the start of the duel.

The battle ends. Mike stands over you, panting, his blade lowered slightly but his gaze unwavering.

"I wasn't able to break your stinky stubbornness today... but I won't stop trying to save you, `<username>`! Not ever!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Mike\|Mike]]

With a swift leap, Mike retreats into the rocky cliffs, fleeing the area. The beach falls dead silent.

"Ugh what a nasty clean brat. Wait. IS THAT A CHEST? GIMME GIMME GIMME!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/The Dark Tome\|The Dark Tome]]
