## 🏃 The Escape: Running Blind
You sprint blindly into the dark tree line of the forest. But as you cross the threshold of the trees, a cold, echoing voice—**The Nightmare**, the exact titanic voice from your dream—booms directly inside your skull:

# "SMELL."

Your [[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Part 1/Others/Curse\|marks]] instantly burst into a violent, sickening greenish-violet glow. A **3-Minute Countdown Timer** appears at the top of the screen.

With the dark, pulsing [H34RT] throbbing in your chest and green smog venting from your pores, you sprint down the root-tangled path while dark whispers begin hissing:

*"To the left, mortal... into the rot... follow the stench if you wish to live..."* -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/The Dark Tome\|a shadowy voice]]

You tumble through the mouth of the hidden cavern, gasping for breath as the beasts outside screech and recoil, choked by the foul odor emanating from both you and the cave.
