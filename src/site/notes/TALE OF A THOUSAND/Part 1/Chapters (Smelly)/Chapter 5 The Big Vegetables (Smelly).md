# Chapter 5: The Big Vegetables
You step under the canopy of the [[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Part 1/Locations/Dark Forest\|Dark Forest]]. The trees are so dense they swallow the sunlight, leaving you in a suffocating twilight. As you try to navigate, the paths seem to shift and twist behind your back—warping like a living maze.

"I smell... Something TRULY YUMMY! We have to find the smell! Oh, we just have tooooo!!!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/The Dark Tome\|The Dark Tome]]

You follow a smelly stench aura through the forest. The trees you get near to instantly rot and wither. There's a point where you pass through some trees and something sparkly falls on your head. You feel nothing. Suddenly, you blink. The camera is now birds view and the forest seems different (Curse dimension)

"Ah... ah... A-TCHOOO! Pepper makes me sneeze heeheehee!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/The Dark Tome\|The Dark Tome]]

"It's story time, little kids!!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Witch\|Witch]]

"Oh great! I love story times! Especially ones about putting stuff in my nose!!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/The Dark Tome\|The Dark Tome]]

"It's time to hear the tale of three little piggies. Once upon a time there were three clean little pigs. An explorer, a spider and a cute little witch. They loved to bathe in the sea and be clean. One day, a dirty stinky wolf came to the forest where they lived. He said 'I'm going to rot the whole forest with my stench!'. The three little piggies didn't wanna get dirty, so they built three houses to keep the dirty out." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Witch\|Witch]]

(You progress through some stages and fight some random explorers [The player used sniff. It's super effective!]. After you defeat them they retreat to the house.)

"The younger pig made a house with sticks and stones. But when the big bad stinky wolf came, he huffed... And he puffed... and he SNIFFED THE WHOLE HOUSE DOWN!!!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Witch\|Witch]]

"I'm beginning to like this story!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/The Dark Tome\|The Dark Tome]]

The Dark Tome summons a giant nose to mega sniff the house down. It also mega sniffs the scents of 8 explorers.

"The middle brother made a house with strings and webs. But when the big nasty stinky wolf came, he huffed... And he puffed... And he SNIFFED THE WHOLE HOUSE DOWN!!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Witch\|Witch]]

"Aww man no scent in these webs." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/The Dark Tome\|The Dark Tome]]

"But when the stinky wolf arrived at the third house, a nasty surprise awaited him. The witch was starting to light a fire, and so when he mega sniffed the house down he would-" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Witch\|Witch]]

"How thoughtful of you. I love me some hot bacon!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/The Dark Tome\|The Dark Tome]]

"No, that's not what I- WHAT ARE YOU DOING?! STOP!!!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Witch\|Witch]]
*The Witch Gets Mega Sniffed*

You exit the curse dimension after The Witch drops her [[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Part 1/Others/PageStone\|SmellStone]]

"Yum yum smells like candy! Now, onward! We must find the other *yummy* smell!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/The Dark Tome\|The Dark Tome]]

After wandering in circles, the path suddenly opens up into a wide, unnatural clearing where the canopy breaks, revealing the gray sky above. Clouds are starting to close in on you. Suddenly, a high-pitched, electronic beeping echoes through the trees.
*BEEP. BEEP. BEEP.*
You follow the sound to the center of the clearing, where a metallic, high-tech [[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Turret\|Turret]] suddenly activates, locking its red laser-sight directly onto your chest.

"Robots? Ugh too clean. They only reek of motor oil" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/The Dark Tome\|The Dark Tome]]
## 🤖 MINIBOSS: The Prototype Sentry
An overworld skirmish begins. As you lower the turret's HP to half, its metallic plates violently shift, expanding until it swells to the size of a small house. A blinding, unstable energy begins to swirl in its barrel, charging a devastating blast.

"Ooooh it's going to sneeze! Why don't you too???" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/The Dark Tome\|The Dark Tome]]

The turret is a split-second away from obliterating you—but right before it fires, a mechanical grappling cable flies out from the treeline, wrapping around the turret's barrel and violently jerking it backward. The turret fires a massive laser beam straight into the sky, safely missing you.

"Whoa there! You almost got completely disintegrated, little fella!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Lyze\|Lyze]]

"Who are you? And why are you tracking me?" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/You\|You]] *(Grip tightening on your weapon)*

A girl in a high-tech lab coat over casual grease-stained clothes steps out from the shadows, casually tossing a wrench into the air. Holographic screens float around her wrist.

"The name's Lyze. I'm a scientist specializing in advanced weapon research and tactical engineering. I've actually been tracking your little... Odour signature for a while. Honestly? You seem like the absolute perfect test subject." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Lyze\|Lyze]]

"Ugh big words make my head go ouchies" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/The Dark Tome\|The Dark Tome]]

"I'm nobody's test subject." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/You\|You]] 
You start to charge an Armpit Flash

"Hey. Stop. I know what you're going to do. Before you do that I've got to warn you, my drone here is equipped with a water nozzle. You shoot, it will too" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Lyze\|Lyze]]

The drone revolving around her closed in on you and points at you with a water gun. You stop charging.

"Listen. I've got a biology experiment I need done. I need to acquire the world's nastiest smell. If you help me, I can help you. In fact, take this watch. I call it The Stinker's Watch. It's equipped with diverse functions to aid you on your journey, the most important one being the water reflector shields." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Lyze\|Lyze]] 

She slaps a sleek digital smart-watch onto your wrist.
 * **Item Acquired:** You obtained the [[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Part 1/Gadgets/Stinker's Watch\|Stinker's Watch]]!

​Take this grappling hook too. You can use it to yank stuff or pull yourself out of puddles. Now hush, let's see if that watch actually keeps you from dissolving." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Lyze\|Lyze]]

She tosses a heavy, sleek metallic gauntlet into your hands.
 * **Item Acquired:** You obtained the [[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Part 1/Gadgets/Grappling Hook\|Grappling Hook]]!

Lyze casually walks over to the giant, humming turret and smacks it with her wrench, forcing its system to reboot.

"There. I've shifted the configurations for its defensive matrix. Consider this a field test—try surviving its water attacks!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Lyze\|Lyze]]
## 🚨 SYSTEM NOTICE: Tutorial Override
>  * **Difficulty Shift:** The Turret now shoots water bullets which will damage your shield. If your shield breaks and water damages you, you immediately die.

You utilize the Grappling Hook to shatter the turret's armor and defeat it (if your shields held up). The machine deactivates, popping open to drop a handful of glowing, metallic gears.
 * **Rewards Unlocked:** You obtained **Gears** (Used at Lyze's workshop for tech upgrades).

"Not bad! Every time you clear a trial or beat my malfunctioning tech, you'll earn Gears to upgrade your arsenal." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Lyze\|Lyze]]

You hear a thunder rumble. It looks like it's about to pour with rain.

"Woah, would you look at those clouds! I don't think your watch can shield you from the rain yet, and I left my spare umbrella back at my headquarters. Hey, come here for a second." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Lyze\|Lyze]]

With a swift tap on her wrist controller, a holographic grid surrounds you both, and you instantly warp away in a flash of blue light.
