# Chapter 3: Green Fields 
### (Intro)
The screen slowly fades out from the burning ruins of your village. The crackle of the fire grows faint, replaced by the heavy, ragged breathing of your character walking through the quiet, moonlit woods.
We cut to a cinematic side-view. Your character is trudging slowly down a dirt path. The sky behind them is stained a toxic orange from the distant, burning town.
Your character stops. You look down at your trembling, ash-stained hands.

*(Voice shaking uncontrollably)*
"I... I sniffed him. Peter was my best friend, and I... I turned him into a scent..." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/You\|You]]

*The heavy leather Tome resting on your hip pulses with a cold, faint glow, its pages rustling slightly against your leg.*

"Mmmm I can still taste him with my nostrils." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/The Dark Tome\|The Dark Tome]]

"You're a psychopath! " -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/You\|You]]

"You can't deny you also like the smell of power." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/The Dark Tome\|The Dark Tome]]

"I..." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/You\|You]]

Your character stands silent for a long moment. Slowly, you clench your trembling fists, swallow the lump in your throat, and continue walking down the path into the dark.
## 🌾 The Green Fields
The gloomy forest path slowly opens up. The oppressive, ash-filled atmosphere dissolves as the screen fades into a bright, almost blindingly vibrant meadow. Peaceful, cheerful RPG music begins to play—a sickeningly happy contrast to the horror you just committed.

Suddenly, strange gelatinous creatures begin hopping out of the tall grass.

"Ewww, lookie! Slimes! They're disgusting but don't even leave a scent!!! Even when they fart!!"  -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/The Dark Tome\|The Dark Tome]]
### 👾 Overworld Combat Phase
 * **Mechanic Change:** Unlike Raider encounters, wild monster skirmishes happen directly in the overworld—no separate Arena transition.
 * **The Reward:** Defeating wild monsters awards **EXP** and items, but adds **0 Scents** to your counter (because monsters have low/no soul value).
## 🎬 Cutscene: An Unwanted Shield
At the end of the valley path, a massive, oversized slime blocks the dirt road, completely asleep. You quietly step forward and raise your blade to deliver a fatal strike.
"Ugh it does not smell. Make it go bye-bye" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/The Dark Tome\|The Dark Tome]]

You swing your blade down with full force—but right before it connects, a flash of steel intercepts your weapon! A loud *CLANG* echoes as a boy your age parries your blow with a traveler's sword, shielding the monster.

"Stop!!!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Mike\|Mike]]

"Huh?!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/You\|You]]

"SNIFFF... Wait. What is that? Ewwww! He smells like soap and sunshine and hugs! GROSS! Get him away from me, he burns my nose holes!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/The Dark Tome\|The Dark Tome]]

"You almost killed it! It was just sleeping!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Mike\|Mike]]

"It's a monster. It's in the way... and I need to- " -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/You\|You]]

"Oh, What's that smell... I mean, sorry, I shouldn't have cut you off. Sorry. You were saying?" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Mike\|Mike]]

The boy sheathes his sword and immediately wraps the giant, squishy slime into a massive, enthusiastic hug. The slime bounces happily.

"Ewww, disgusting! Not smelly disgusting. Just eww disgusting." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/The Dark Tome\|The Dark Tome]]

*Looking up from the slime, completely ignoring your cold expression*
"Hey, what's your name anyway?" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Mike\|Mike]]

"Why should I tell you?" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/You\|You]]

"Because I need to know what to call my new best friend, obviously!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Mike\|Mike]]

​(Your character flinches slightly at the word "best friend," taking a small step back. A quick visual flicker of Peter's body appears over Mike for half a second before vanishing.)

The sheer, unbothered optimism of his answer catches you completely off guard. You hesitate.

​"I'm not your friend. I don't... do friends." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/You\|You]]

​"Hi not-my-friend, I'm Mike!"-[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Mike\|Mike]]

"I don't care." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/You\|You]]

"So, where are you traveling to?" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Mike\|Mike]]

"The [[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Part 1/Locations/Pirate Docks\|Pirate Docks]]." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/You\|You]]

"Oh, wow, that place is incredibly dangerous! Full of cutthroats and thieves! Tell you what—let me come with you! I can protect you! Plus... well, honestly, if we walk together, maybe the coastal ocean breeze will... uh... help air out whatever happened to your laundry! Win-win!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Mike\|Mike]]
## ⚔️ Party Status Update: Mike Joined the Team!
*(Note: You do not get a choice. He is trailing behind you now. Deal with it.)*
### The "Pacifist" Shield Mechanic:
 * **Gameplay Restriction:** As long as Mike is active in your party, you **cannot attack** wild creatures in the overworld.
 * **The Penalty:** If you try to swing your weapon at a wild monster, Mike will instantly dash in front of your blade, parry the attack, and hit you with a non-lethal shield bash that **ragdolls your character onto the floor**.
 * **The Tome's Reaction:** Every time you get ragdolled, the Tome mocks you like a 3 year old:
>  "HA-HA! LOSER! You fell down! Look at the funny worm on the ground! Do it again, do it again!"
>   "POINT AND LAUGH! HE GOT BONKED BY THE BLOND BOY! WAAAH, WAAAH, CRYBABY FELL OVER!"
>   "Get up, silly! Stop eating grass! Grass tastes yucky!"
