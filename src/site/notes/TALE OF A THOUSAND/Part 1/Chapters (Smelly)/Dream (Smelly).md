## Phase 2: The Awakening of Power
Suddenly, the hundreds of souls from the pillars line the area. Their eyes snap open, glowing with absolute terror, and they rush toward the player in a frenzied stampede.

The player barely even flinches. As the swarm of souls rushes close, the suffocating wave of the player's putrid aura hits them like a physical wall. Every single soul immediately chokes, clutches their throat, and falls flat to the ground, writhing and paralyzed by the unbearable stench.

The player looks like they are charging something immense.

Slowly, the player raises their arm—exposing their armpit directly toward the sea of fallen souls. 

A catastrophic, blinding beam of noxious energy erupts straight from the armpit, tearing through the chamber and disintegrating every last soul into dust with its devastating, putrid odor.

As the blinding blast sweeps through the crowd, the camera pulls tight and lingers directly on one specific soul collapsed near the front: **[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Peter\|Peter]]**.

His eyes are wide with shock and horror as the foul beam washes over him, completely vaporizing him before the screen bleeds white.
