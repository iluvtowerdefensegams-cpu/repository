# The Lab & The Grand Map

"Come in, come in! Welcome, test subject. I have some massive upgrades to discuss with you. Step over to the scanner... Perfect! Let's see what your smell is actually made of." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Lyze\|Lyze]]

"Downloading the smell logs now... and, wow. These are incredible results. Here is how your odour data breaks down: your smell classifies itself into two categories—Scents and SweatXP. The more you get for each one in battle, the more your combat proficiency level increases, unlocking brand-new, lethal abilities! 

But this is just a baseline. I need you out there gathering more real-world smell data. I also wanted to ask you about—wait. What on earth is *that*?" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Lyze\|Lyze]]

## 🏘️ EXPLORING THE HQ: Meet the Locals

You are free to explore the sprawling base. The plaza is teeming with life.

### 🍞 Brandon the Baker (The Kitchen)
*Inspired by Cooking Mama*
You walk into a sweet-smelling bakery, only to be immediately clocked in the head with a heavy cast-iron frying pan.

"DON'T JUST STAND THERE SWEATING! Hands washed, apron on! If you want my bread, you're going to earn it. Do NOT make me whack you again!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Brandon The Baker\|Brandon The Baker]]
* **The Mechanic:** To unlock cooking and purchase food/recipes, you must complete high-stress, rhythmic cooking lessons. Mess up the timing, and Brandon *will* hit you with the pan again. You can also work shifts here to earn quick cash.

### ⚒️ Smithy Steve (The Forge)
*Inspired by Minecraft's Steve*
Standing by a massive anvil is a blocky, incredibly muscular blacksmith staring intensely into a furnace.

"I... am Steve. As a child, I yearned for the mines. The deep, dark blocks called to me. Now, I shape the metal." -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Steve The BlackSmith-Smithy Steve\|Steve The BlackSmith-Smithy Steve]]
* **The Mechanic:** Steve can upgrade your sword and bow using materials you find. He specializes in highly volatile, gunpowder-related gadgets. Be careful—his upgrade sequences have a tendency to end in a loud, smoking **TNT explosion** that leaves him completely unfazed.

### 🤖 Scratch (The Workshop)
*Inspired by the Scratch learning platform*
In a side workshop filled with orange gears and wires, Lyze's first apprentice is frantically programming a humanoid chassis.

"Welcome to the Scratchpad! I build the future! You help me find high-grade machine parts, and I'll let you deploy your very own combat robots!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Scratch\|Scratch]]
* **The Mechanic:** Completing his side quests unlocks **Robots** (special summons with their own programmable skill trees). 
* **The Minigame:** He also features a customization terminal where you can design a custom machine... only for it to immediately turn on you in a "Design a machine to thrash your own ass" Deltarune-style combat challenge.

### 🗑️ Scrappy (The Back Alley Dumpster)
Scrappy's lair is empty but for a lone [[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Part 1/Others/PageStone\|SmellStone]]

Dev notes for the HQ: 
- Interactive Weapon Proficiency Tree: A Watch UI panel displaying current level progression across Free-hand, Melee, Ranged, and Arcane.
- Brandon's Frying Pan Rhythm Widget: A quick-time rhythm bar simulating Brandon's high-stress cooking lessons (with pan whacks on missed notes).
- Steve's Volatile Forge Risk Meter: A visual forge console calculating TNT explosion odds vs. weapon stat gains when upgrading.
- Scratch's "Thrash Your Own Ass" Simulator: A custom robot design terminal that generates a boss challenge based on your own design choices.

## 🎪 Talking to Salariem the Shopkeeper

Salariem's shop is wide open, decorated with colorful banners. The moment you step close, Salariem begins playing a wild, incredibly catchy tune on his accordion, dancing on his heels.

🎶 *"Step right up, step right up! Be it soap or shiny rings, Salariem sells the finest things! Welcome, my friend! What can I do for you on this glorious day?"* -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Salariem The Shopkeeper\|Salariem The Shopkeeper]] (subject to change)

"What do you know about the theme park on the way to Wignus Tower?" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/You\|You]]

*Instantly stops playing, gasping dramatically, and begins talking at a blistering, hyper-fast speed, gesticulating wildly with his hands*
"Oh! You mean **[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Part 1/Locations/Super Fun Land\|Super Fun Land]]**! Where the fun *never* ends! Or, well, where the fun *used* to never end! It's run by my dear, wonderful, absolutely cuckoo-in-the-brain old friend, **Septer**!  

Though, sad story, really—nobody has set foot in that park for years. Septer went completely off his rocker! He filled the entire place with automated, killer steam-robots, and he's running the whole park using a glowing Chaos Beacon as a battery! 

You're going there to take it, aren't you? Oh, I knew it! If you fight him, do me a favor and try not to break his Sceptre—I absolutely *adore* his Sceptre. Might even buy it off you if you bring it back! But please don't get sweat on it.

Oh, and one trade secret, my friend! Septer never lets anyone leave unless they buy the ultimate prize from his gift shop—the Surprise Toy! You may think it's just a silly trinket, but between you and me? Inside that box is the only stamped Golden Exit Pass out of that madhouse. Two-for-one special: play his games, buy the toy, and you get the toy AND your ticket out! Just don't let him catch you trying to leave without it!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/Salariem The Shopkeeper\|Salariem The Shopkeeper]]

## 🛣️ The Road to Super Fun Land

You leave the safety of the HQ, following the map coordinates. The sky grows dim as you trek down the mountain path.

## ⚔️ The Bandit Encounter: 
- Along the rocky road, four coughing bandits block the misty path, muttering about a "poisoner's hideout." Trapped by toxic fog, they turn, spot you, and draw their rusty weapons in desperation.
- You don't offer words or mercy. You only offer smell. Their scents rise in pale wisps, vanishing into the Tome's open nostrils.
- "Yummmmy!" -[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Characters/The Dark Tome\|The Dark Tome]] mutters, snapping shut.
## 🏔️ The Trial Cave: 
- Nearby, you spot a glowing cave entrance—another one of **Lyze's Trials**. It is guarded by high-level monsters. Beating the trial doesn't yield scents (as they are artificial constructs), but completing it rewards you with valuable upgrade materials.

Wiping the dust from your blade, you stand before the rusted, towering iron gates of *[[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Part 1/Locations/Super Fun Land\|Super Fun Land]]*. The creepy, distorted sound of carnival music echoes from within...
