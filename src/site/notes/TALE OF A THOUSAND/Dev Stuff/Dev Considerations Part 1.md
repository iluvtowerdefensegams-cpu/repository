## 🧭 The Three Trials

### Trial 1: "Once Upon a Tyme" ([[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Part 1/Chapters (Skotos)/Chapter 1 Raiders\|TALE OF A THOUSAND/Part 1/Chapters (Skotos)/Chapter 1 Raiders]] — The Library)
* **Location:** The dusty forgotten wing of the Library archives in Chapter 1.
* **The Discovery:** Tucked between ancient encyclopedias is a book with a spine crafted from brass clockwork gears titled: *Another Tale*.
* **The Puzzle:** Opening the book reveals the opening inscription:
  > *"Once upon a Tyme, there was a fool who thought every second was his to spend..."*
* **The Mechanic:** The pages do not turn from right to left; they turn based on a clock riddle (e.g., turning pages forward by 3 hours, back by 6 minutes, aligning the bookmarks to 12:00).
* **The Reward:** The book snaps shut, leaving a glowing brass pendulum brand on your palm: **Chronos Key I**. A distant voice whispers:  
  > *"Seek the tower that pierces the seconds."*

### Trial 2: "The Chrono-Maze" ([[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Part 1/Chapters (Skotos)/Chapter 10 The Sword Locked In A Tower\|TALE OF A THOUSAND/Part 1/Chapters (Skotos)/Chapter 10 The Sword Locked In A Tower]] — Tyme Tower)
* **Location:** Lower sublevels of Wignus Tower before boarding the lift down.
* **The Environment:** You step through a flickering chronological rift where gear teeth grind inside stone walls and floor tiles phase in and out of reality.

  * Rooms loop infinitely unless traversed following cardinal clock directions (12 o'clock, 3 o'clock, 6 o'clock, 9 o'clock).
  * Specific barriers only unseal when the background pendulum rhythm hits a designated chime.
* **The Climax:** At the core sits a frozen brass effigy clutching a cracked pocket watch. Touching it sets the hands ticking again: **Chronos Key II**.  
  > *"Two hands aligned. Only the witching hour remains in the woods where compasses die."*

* **Requirement:** Must have defeated **The Dark Puppet** in Chapter 24 and possess both Chronos Keys.

### 🎮 The 3 Boss Phases

#### 🗡️ Phase 1: The Master Duelist
* **Combat Style:** Pure, relentless swordplay. Tyme wields a twin-pronged blade shaped like antique clock hands.
* **Difficulty:** Plays like a classic brutal humanoid boss—faster, tighter, and hit-for-hit harder than Mike's Phase 1 final battle. No gimmicks yet, just pure parry/dodge checks and punishing frame traps.
* **Scaling Difficulty (Time Debt):** Every major boss and NPC killed throughout your save file adds extra phantom after-strikes to his combos, cutting your recovery windows.

---

#### ⏳ Phase 2: The Time-Lock Mindgame
Tyme begins stopping you in place using his signature **Time-Lock Rings**. Players have 2 seconds or less to react, but he forces you to read the visual cue:

1. **🟡 Golden Clocks (The Mash Check):**
   * A gold clock ring wraps around your character. Screen turns sepia; inputs freeze.
   * **Solution:** Mash dodge/attack inputs as fast as possible to shatter the stasis. Even when mashing perfectly, you have under 2 seconds to dodge his oncoming slash.
2. **🔴 Red Clocks (The Patience Trap):**
   * A crimson clock appears overhead with a ticking 1-second countdown.
   * **The Trap:** Mashing is forbidden! Each key press **extends the lock by +0.1s**.
   * **Solution:** Hands off the keyboard/controller. Wait out the freeze in absolute silence, then parry the strike the exact split-second the red clock expires.

---

#### 🛑 Phase 3: The Temporal Freeze (Ragequit Engine)
Designed specifically to shatter player muscle memory and exploit the game client:
* **The Mechanic:** Tyme winds up a lethal, arena-wide fatal cleave. A visible 3-second strike timer begins.
* **The Freeze:** At second 1.0, the game client itself is forced into an artificial lag/freeze state for 2.0 to 2.5 seconds (the screen hitches, audio stutters, models freeze mid-air).
* **The Snapback:** Right when the blade is milliseconds from cleaving the player, the client abruptly unfreezes. The player has a fraction of a second to react and execute a frame-perfect parry or dodge.
