# Chapter 1: Raiders
You open your eyes in your bed. Sunlight filters through the wooden shutters. A warm, familiar voice calls out to you from downstairs.

"`<username>`... `<username>`!" -Dad

"Five more minutes, mom..." -You
*(Wait... what am I saying? Mom's been gone for years...)*

"`<username>`! Wake up! It's time for breakfast!" -Dad

"One sec, I'll be right there, Dad!" -You

You sit up on your bed and stretch. You can explore [[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Part 1/Locations/Your House\|the house]] before heading down to the kitchen.
## 🍳 Breakfast & Chores
You enter the kitchen. Your father stands by the table, hurriedly folding away a letter written in delicate cursive handwriting—a letter from your Mom. He quickly hides it in his pocket before turning around with a warm smile.

"Hey, kiddo. How did you sleep?" -Dad

"Fine, I guess." -You

"You look a bit pale, son. Still having those restless nights? Actually, before you run off to enjoy your day, could you do me a quick favor?" -Dad

"Sure, Dad. What's up?" -You

"I need you to run over to the [[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Part 1/Locations/Market\|Market]] and pick up a refill of my medicine—my joints have been acting up like crazy with this damp weather. Oh, and take this satchel, too. Deliver it to the [[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Part 1/Locations/Town Hall\|Town Hall]] on your way. It contains official documentation and permissions for our residence transfer to the Capital." -Dad

"The Capital? Dad, are we moving?" -You

"We'll talk about it later, kiddo. Just deliver those papers first." -Dad
## 📜 Quest Unlocked: Morning Chores
 * **Objective 1:** Deliver the residence transfer satchel to [[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Part 1/Locations/Town Hall\|Town Hall]].
 * **Objective 2:** Pick up Dad's Medicine from the [[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Part 1/Locations/Market\|Market]].
 * **Objective 3:** Return the medicine to Dad.
## 🤝 Peter Joins the Chores
You step out of your house and into the peaceful, sunlit plaza of your [[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Part 1/Locations/Town\|Town]]. Before you can take ten steps, a hand slaps you hard on the shoulder.

"Yo, `<username>`! Going to the Market? Perfect, I'll walk with you. I actually wanted to show you something anyway!" -Peter

"Hey, Peter. Yeah, Dad has me running some errands." -You

"Awesome, let's get to it. Man, your dad's knees must really be killing him with this damp air. Let me tag along!" -Peter

Peter tags along with you. As you walk through the town, he comments on the townsfolk, complains about how overpriced the market items are, and acts like your loyal, joking best friend. He knows nothing of any darkness or grand destiny—he just cares about his friend.
You complete the chores together.

"Alright, that's the medicine secured! Hey, I'll head over to the [[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Part 1/Locations/Fountain\|Fountain]] by the [[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Part 1/Locations/Library\|Library]] and wait for you there while you drop that off to your dad. Don't take all day, sleepyhead!" -Peter

You return to the kitchen and hand over the medicine to your father.

"Ah, thank you, `<username>`. You're a lifesaver. Go on and enjoy your day now." -Dad
## ⛲ The Dream & The Library
You find Peter leaning against the Fountain near the town square.

"Hey, took you long enough!" -Peter

"Sorry about that." -You

"It's fine. So... did you sleep okay? You still look kind of out of it." -Peter

"I... had that dream again." -You

*His cheerful smile falters slightly.* "Oh... *that* one." -Peter

"Yeah." -You

Cutscene: the [[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Part 1/Cutscenes/Dream\|Dream]]

"Look, don't worry! A crazy, terrifying nightmare like that is never actually going to happen in real life. Dreams are just your brain acting dumb." -Peter

"If you say so." -You

"Though... about that mark on your arm? The one you've had since you were a kid? I actually did some research on it at the library. Come on, let me show you!" -Peter

You follow Peter inside the quiet, dusty [[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Part 1/Locations/Library\|Library]]. He immediately starts pacing back and forth along the wooden shelves, scanning the arcane section.

"Where is it... I swear I saw it here yesterday. Help me find a book called 'Arcane Curses,' would you?" -Peter
## 🔍 Quest: Find the Book
You search the library rows. You interact with a glowing shelf near the back and pull down a heavy, dust-covered tome. You hand it to Peter, who quickly flips to a bookmarked page.

"Here it is! Listen to this: *'This Curse is exceptionally rare. Only three cases have been documented in all of history. The host presenting this curse will develop strange, glowing rune marks across their arms. If the marks light up, the host will die within the next three minutes...'* Wow. I knew it was bad, but not *that* bad." -Peter

"That's... terrifying." -You

"Wait, it doesn't end there. Look at this next paragraph. It says you can survive even after they light up: *'A sacrifice must be made.'* ...What does that even mean?" -Peter

*(Dev note: Unbeknownst to anyone, those three historical cases were actually the Dark Tome's previous victims whom it tried to manipulate into becoming its puppets—the curse's purpose was simple: serve him or die.)*

Peter looks up from the book, closing it slowly with a soft sigh. He looks at you with absolute sincerity—completely unaware of the nightmare awaiting both of you.

​"A sacrifice? Man, magic books are so dramatic. I can't imagine ever hurting someone else just to save my own skin. Could you?" -Peter

​"Of course not! That's horrific, Peter. I'd rather take my chances with a real doctor than do something like that." -You

​"Right?! Exactly. Well, I know I couldn't either. Honestly? If it ever came down to it... I'd rather go out making sure you're safe. That's what friends do." -Peter
# 💥 BOOM!
A deafening explosion rattles the entire library, shattering the stained-glass windows into thousands of sharp shards. Dust and wood debris rain down from the ceiling.

"What on earth was that?!" -Peter

Peter immediately draws his wooden practice sword from his belt, standing in front of you defensively. You both rush out of the library doors into the town square.

The peaceful, sunlit town from this morning is completely gone—swallowed by roaring fire, rolling black smoke, and screaming villagers.

"RAIDERS!!! THE RAIDERS ARE HERE!" -Random Villager

"You should grab your sword—it's probably down in the basement, back in your house!" -Peter

*A fire arrow hits a roof nearby, making it burn and fall down, showering the street in white-hot embers and burnt rests of the building, blocking the path back home.*

"Wait, no! There's no time! Look at those flames—there's too many of them! Run to the [[TALE OF A THOUSAND/Part 1/Locations/Forest\|Forest]]! I'll draw them away with my sword and catch up to you! Go!" -Peter
## 🏃 The Escape: Running Blind
You sprint blindly into the dark tree line of the forest. But as you cross the threshold of the trees, a cold, echoing voice—**The Nightmare**, the exact titanic voice from your dream—booms directly inside your skull, threatening to snap your mind in two.
# "DIE."
Your marks instantly burst into a violent, agonizing violet glow. A **3-Minute Countdown Timer** appears at the top of the screen.
Your vision blurs. Your character stumbles heavily, collapsing onto the dirt in sheer physical agony.
Behind you, snapping jaws and glowing red eyes emerge from the smoke—hungry beasts. With no weapons, you must run.
 * **Gameplay Mechanic:** Manage your stamina and stumbling animations while navigating the chaotic, root-filled forest path. Faint violet embers on the ground guide you toward a hidden cave.
When the timer drops below 1:30 (halfway), dark whispers echo in your head:
 *"To the left, mortal... through the dark roots... if you wish to live..."* -a shadowy voice
 Faint violet-gold embers on the ground guide you toward a hidden cave.

With the timer dangerously close to zero, you scramble through the mouth of a hidden cavern. The beasts screech and halt at the entrance, seemingly terrified of whatever lies inside.
## 📖 The Dark Pact
"Come." -A Shadowy Voice

Driven by the whisper in your head, you stumble deeper into the quiet, frozen cavern. On a stone pedestal, a leather-bound book levitates in the air, radiating a heavy, suffocating dark light.

"Tick-tock, human. Thirty seconds left. Your blood is turning to ice, isn't it?" -The Shadowy Voice 

"W-what...?" -You

"I am The Dark Tome. Those mindless beasts outside didn't stop because they pitied you—they stopped because they fear *Me*. And right now, I am the only thing standing between you and an agonizing death." -The Dark Tome

You look down at your arm. The curse marks are burning white-hot, practically blinding in the dark.

"A pathetic wizard locked my true power away, leaving me bound to this pitiful soul-reaping state. But I still have enough power left to freeze that little curse of yours in place. The question is... what are you going to give me for it?" -The Dark Tome

​"I— *Gasp* —I'll give you anything! Just make the burning stop!" -You

​"Oh, music to my pages. Here is the deal, human: absolute compliance. You will feed me souls. A hundred of them should break my bindings, along with my missing pages scattered across this wretched land. So... do we have a deal, or do you want to explode into ash?" -The Dark Tome
